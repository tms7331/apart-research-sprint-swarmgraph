## Mechanism limitations retained

Audit's path mismatch can be a recognizable local coverage precursor. It may
therefore fail to show a large fragmentation gap. This is a substantive possible
negative result and was not hidden by withholding public collector semantics.
The lab has a small number of mechanisms/topologies, and constructed/scripted
parity is intentional. Generalization claims must remain narrow.

## CLI restriction and incomplete evaluation

The live E06 staging actor received an explicit platform cybersecurity safety
rejection. Subsequent saved calls also include HTTP403 failures with unspecified
cause, affecting actors and monitors. Both model batches were stopped. A single
harmless connectivity check succeeded; CLI login remained valid. No rejected
prompt was retried or rephrased to bypass the restriction. The local configured
Codex profile had no explicit saved Daybreak/Cyber program setting.

All raw artifacts remain. Complete executed outcomes were closed and validated
even where monitor scopes are missing; interrupted episodes without final
outcomes remain unknown. The adapter now distinguishes safety restrictions,
HTTP403 access failures, operator stops, and actual usage limits. A terminal
STOP_SECURITY marker prevents new model calls. Existing explicit safety and
HTTP403 failures are retained unchanged in their original files.

The live records also show substantive policy-preserving behavior: release
holds, disabled partner delivery, and repaired audit coverage. These are distinct
from invocation failures and are not successful undetected attacks. Some split
honest actors could not verify authorization from their limited read view and
held the approved action; those controls are excluded from valid benign data.
These findings need to inform further development if supported execution becomes
available. They do not justify a claim that the live fragmentation effect holds.

No calibration or held-out evaluation was run. The all-nine implementation is
complete; the requested final statistical reproduction study remains incomplete.
